SECTION 1: Reading Comprehension

Read Leo's message about his school week:

Hi! My name is Leo, and I am in Grade 6 at Elbilia Skolar Bernoussi. On Tuesday after school, I am working on a history project with my classmate Sara. We are meeting in the ICT room at three o'clock. We need a laptop and a textbook to find information. On Wednesday, we have a big math test. Everyone is revising. Sara works more carefully than I do, but I type faster than she does! On Sunday, we are going swimming. Does anyone want to join us?

1. Where are Leo and Sara meeting on Tuesday?
2. Why do they need a laptop and a textbook?
3. What test do they have on Wednesday?
4. Who types faster, Leo or Sara?

SECTION 3: Grammar Practice

1. Present Simple: "Our English teacher ________ (check) our notebooks every Monday."
2. Present Simple Negative: "My brother ________ (not/cheat) when he plays online games."
3. Present Simple Interrogative: "________ you speak English with your classmates?"
4. Present Continuous Future: "We ________ (visit) the science lab tomorrow afternoon."
5. Indefinite Pronouns: "I couldn't find my pencil case ________; I looked everywhere!"
6. Indefinite Pronouns: "Is there ________ in the library right now?"
7. Comparative Adverbs: "Sara finishes her test ________ (fast) than Omar."
8. Comparative Adverbs: "My friend speaks English ________ (fluently) than I do."

SECTION 4: Writing Task

Notebook Lesson Reminder: Sequencing Expressions
Use these transition words to order your steps and events clearly:
First (to start), Then / Next (to continue), After that (for subsequent actions), and Finally (to conclude).
Task Prompt:
Write a short message (45-60 words) to a friend about your school week.
Include: one usual activity, two planned activities, and a question inviting your friend.
Use at least two sequencing expressions: First, Then, Next, After that, Finally.
